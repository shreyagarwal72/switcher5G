Bug report checklist

Include these so we can reproduce the problem:

  • Device. Brand and model, for example Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra.
  • Android version. Include One UI, HyperOS or other skin if you know it.
  • Carrier and country. 5G SA support differs by network.
  • Engine used. Shizuku (with version), Root, Direct ADB or RadioInfo.
  • App version. Shown on the About screen.
  • Steps and result. What mode you picked, what you expected, what happened.
  • Screenshot. Include any error message exactly as shown.
