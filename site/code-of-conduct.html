Code of conduct

Be kind. Be useful. Keep it welcoming.

Last updated 24 October 2026

Switcher 5G is a small project run by volunteers. We want its Telegram group, GitHub issues and any other project space to be friendly and useful for everyone, whatever their experience or background.

What we expect

  • Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning about Shizuku, ADB and root for the first time.
  • Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, harassment, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Stay on topic and be constructive. Give details when you report a problem. Say thanks when someone helps.
  • Keep it safe. Do not share anyone’s private information. Do not post malware, cracked software or anything illegal.
  • Be honest about risk. Do not push people to root or change settings without telling them what could go wrong.

What is not okay

  • Personal attacks, threats, stalking or unwanted sexual attention.
  • Spam, scams or advertising unrelated to the project.
  • Pretending to be a maintainer or sharing fake APKs.
  • Repeating a behavior after being asked to stop.

Enforcement

Maintainers and group admins may edit or remove comments, close issues, mute, or ban people who break these rules. We try to give one clear warning first, unless the behavior is serious.

Reporting a problem

Message a maintainer or admin on Telegram @championworkspace. Reports are handled privately. PLACEHOLDER: add a private contact email for conduct reports

Scope

These rules apply in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
